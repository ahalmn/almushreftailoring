A FEW USEFUL DETAILS
Before you enquire.
How do I order?
Choose a style and prepare an enquiry. Our team will discuss availability, sizing, price, payment and delivery with you before an order is confirmed.
Can I ask for a custom design?
Yes. Tell us your preferred silhouette, fabric, colour and embroidery details. Feasibility, quantity and timing are confirmed after reviewing your requirements.
Do you supply wholesale?
We welcome retail shop, wholesale and importer enquiries. Share the products, quantity, destination and budget you have in mind for an individual quotation.
Are prices and delivery confirmed online?
Prices, stock, production lead time, delivery charges and any applicable taxes are confirmed in your quotation. This catalogue does not accept online payments.
