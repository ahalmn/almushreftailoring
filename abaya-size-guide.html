How to measure

A tape measure and a well-fitting Abaya can help you check the details.

1

Choose your footwear

Stand naturally in the shoes you intend to wear. Your footwear affects where the hem will fall.

2

Shoulder to desired hem

Measure from the top of your shoulder to your preferred hem position. Keep the hem clear of the floor and compare this length with the chart.

3

Confirm room to move

Check bust, hip and sleeve measurements against the chosen design. Tell us your preferred fit so we can discuss the garment width.

Choose your fit

Length and width are separate measurements.

Loose & flowy

Request more room through the bust and hips. A wider cut creates a looser fit; the length size can stay the same.

A closer fit

Ask for the garment’s chest, hip and sleeve measurements. Allow enough ease for comfortable movement.

Wearing heels

Measure with your intended heels and confirm the hem position. Extra length or custom sizing may be needed.

Common questions

Is my height enough to choose a size?

Height gives an approximate starting point. Your shoulder-to-hem length, footwear, body measurements and the garment’s cut are also needed to confirm fit.

What if I am between two length sizes?

Send your shoulder-to-hem measurement and preferred hem position. The team can help you compare the available lengths and discuss alterations or custom sizing.

Do all Abayas use numbered sizes?

Some designs use length numbers such as 52, 54 and 56; others may use lettered sizes or a separate width size. Always check the measurements of the specific design.

What does bust width mean?

A garment’s flat bust width is measured from side to side when laid flat. It differs from full bust circumference. Ask which measurement is being quoted before comparing sizes.

Can I request a custom size?

Contact AL MUSHREF with your measurements, design and quantity. Custom sizing, feasibility, price and timing are confirmed after review.

Still not sure?

Share your height, shoulder-to-hem measurement, preferred fit and footwear with our team. We can discuss the right measurements for your chosen design.
